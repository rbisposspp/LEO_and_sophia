About the Leo & Sofia Learning App

This lab was designed to keep practice simple, communicative, and motivating.

Lab points: 0 Actions completed: 0 Daily streak: 0

This application is designed to help English as a Second Language (ESL) learners improve their vocabulary related to descriptions and personality, and practice basic English grammar in an engaging way through the journey of our characters, Leo and Sofia.

We believe in learning through context and interaction. This app aims to provide a communicative approach to language acquisition.

How to Use This App:

  • Home (Vocabulary): Start here to get familiar with key adjectives by seeing visual contrasts.
  • The Story: Follow Leo and Sofia's friendship through 10 interactive scenes with images and audio.
  • Grammar Practice: Test your understanding with unscramble exercises and a quiz based on the story.

Navigate through the sections using the main menu at the top of each page.

Inspired by principles of Communicative Language Teaching and RODRIGO SIAS teaching experience.
